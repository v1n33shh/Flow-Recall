FlowRecall needs a newer browser engine

The app runs inside Android System WebView, and the version on this device is too old to draw FlowRecall correctly. Rather than show you a half-broken screen, it stops here.

Updating that one component fixes it. Your decks, reviews and reading position are all still on this device and untouched.

Update Android System WebView Use FlowRecall in your browser instead

If that button does nothing, this device may not have the Play Store. Any recent Chrome or Firefox on this phone can open flowrecall.app and everything except the nightly reminder works there.
